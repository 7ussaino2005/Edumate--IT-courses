About us

Edumate - IT Courses is an educational platform for Information and Communication Technology. We are a team of four young Bahrainis specializing in information technology, united by the idea of creating an exceptional educational project that offers quality, speed, and reliability, aiming to perform better and help students have the best experience ever, driven by our love for assisting students with their studies since childhood. Our main idea is to provide the easiest service with good quality experience in order to guarantee the results.

Hardships and Challanges we faced

Every beginning can be difficult, but the challenge was not only in building and developing the website; the difficulty was also that we were university students at the start of our academic journey. We had continuous exams and assignments to complete, and some courses had projects that required full concentration, which made it hard for us to balance everything but every time we used to say that we could overcome the toughest challenges and problems. And we always said that if after all this we reach what we want, then it will be worth it.

Solution to the challanges we faced

We always had faith that we can pass all the hardships that we will we are challenging no matter how difficult it is . therefore, we decided that we will devide our tasks into two hours of work on days of the week and four hours on the weekend and we divided the work into two groups: one group on building and developing the website, and the second group to will find the solutions to previous problems we faced . This is what succeeded in allowing us to complete the project successfully. The remarkable thing about the matter is that we agreed with influencers and some celebrities to promote our developed project. As a result, many students registered with us, in addition to some retired university professors who assisted us and increased their source of income.

What we have learned

we have gained and learned a lot thanks to our passion,
The first thing that we have learned is that teamwork can help us in finishing the tasks easiely. Furthermore, we have gained more experience in Information technology from diffrent majors and the most important thing is that we learned how to divide our tasks between us and plan for our work professionally.
